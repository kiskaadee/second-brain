## Architecture & Design Decisions

### Strict Direction of Integration (`main` $\rightarrow$ `refactor/appctl-rework` $\rightarrow$ `main`)
Treating `appctl-v2` as a hard prerequisite for Phase 1 and Phase 2 would stall necessary infrastructure realignments (mail domain migration, GitOps sandboxing). Conversely, continuous bidirectional merging between `main` and the refactor branch risks thrashing and merge conflicts in rapidly changing files. By adopting a strict one-way contract flow—authoring `core_manifest.py` on `main`, cherry-picking only the contract commit into the worktree, adapting and verifying hermetically, promoting the file, and then merging back to `main`—both tracks maintain complete velocity and clean boundaries until convergence at P3.
