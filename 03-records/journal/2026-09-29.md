### Decoupling Investigation and Documentation Treatment Skills
- Decoupled the monolithic investigation skill into two cooperating, independent global capabilities:
  - **`engineering-investigation`**: Purely procedural troubleshooting following the scientific method (Observation $\to$ Verification) and 6-part interactive checkpoints, terminating cleanly at runtime recovery.
  - **`documentation-router`**: Second-order knowledge classifier and gatekeeper that inspects session context, determines whether durable knowledge preservation is warranted, classifies knowledge by epistemic role (not activity), enforces anti-overlap invariants, and coordinates modular authoring specifications.
- Established the principle that `Discussion ──informs──> ADR ──constrains──> Plan` describes **optional information flow and authority relationships, not a mandatory documentation pipeline**.
- Authored four modular authoring reference specifications: `discussion-authoring.md`, `adr-authoring.md`, `plan-authoring.md`, and `debug-record-authoring.md`.
- Synchronized canonical agent skill definitions in [`05-agents/skills/`](../../05-agents/skills/) and updated global rules in `~/.gemini/config/AGENTS.md`.

### Agent Skill Engineering & Skill-Builder Meta-Skill Creation
- Conducted an in-depth empirical investigation of Matt Pocock's skills repository (`mattpocock/skills`), studying skill anatomy, invocation modes, operational state machines, negative boundaries, progressive disclosure, and context load economics.
- Formalized **Knowledge Articles** as an explicit epistemic document type in [`documentation-router`](../../05-agents/skills/documentation-router/README.md), authoring the specification `references/knowledge-authoring.md` based on a side-by-side learner voice and rhetorical restraint.
- Authored the durable knowledge article [How to Build Effective Agent Skills](../../04-learning/knowledge/methods/building-effective-agent-skills.md), explaining why essay prompts fail, introducing the assembly jig / airlock mental model, and codifying the single observable proof heuristic.
- Derived the Skill-Engineering Methodology based on 4 structural sizing archetypes (Reference, Transformation, Procedural Workflow, Interactive Elicitation) and a 4-tier epistemic architecture (Engineering Invariants, Platform Constraints, Contextual Heuristics, Authoring Quality).
- Designed and implemented the [`skill-builder`](../../05-agents/skills/skill-builder.md) meta-skill (deployed to `~/.gemini/config/skills/skill-builder/` and `/build-skill`) as an interactive coach with a mandatory Design Review Gate.
- Dogfooded `skill-builder` against existing skills (`engineering-investigation`, `engineering-tutor`, `documentation-router`, `git-commit`), validating its false-positive rejection and identifying high-signal refinements.
- Strengthened `engineering-investigation` with Evidence Before Hypothesis, Traceable Diagnostic Cleanup, and Sensitive Data Redaction invariants.
