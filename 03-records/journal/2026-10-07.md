## Mistakes, Gaps & Learning Edges

### `amc-solutions-seo`
- **Achieved (Epistemic Separation in Pre-Discovery Exploration)**: Mastered the necessity of isolating raw empirical observations (`evidence/`) from interpretive analysis (`analysis/`) and stakeholder unknowns (`context/`). Avoided the common failure mode of jumping directly into cosmetic redesigns or unwanted dependencies before diagnosing whether the client's bottleneck is discoverability, information architecture, conversion pathways, or business prioritization.

### `skills`
- **Achieved (Ledger Immutability & Stateful Provenance)**: Solved the architectural tension between an append-only, immutable event ledger (`commit-log.csv`) and consumption tracking. Identified why `git log --since` fails (date boundaries are non-authoritative and blind to review progress) and verified that full 40-character commit SHAs in `assessed_commits` (with unioning during reconciliation) provide an immutable, idempotent provenance boundary.

### `homelab`
- **Achieved (Control Plane Decoupling & Typed Metadata)**: Masterfully articulated the liabilities of legacy ad-hoc shell execution sourcing `/run/secrets/`. Formulated key architectural principles:
  1. *Transport vs. Authority*: SSH or socket transport access does not equal application-level authorization.
  2. *Least-Privilege Secret Resolution*: `SecretProvider` boundary resolves secret references on demand without polluting process environments.
  3. *Typed Invariants*: Types express invariants and enable offline unit tests, while runtime integration tests remain essential for authentication, socket permissions, and reconciliation.
  4. *Git as Truth*: Git remains the sole authoritative source of desired deployment state; the daemon reconciles and executes rather than becoming a competing source of truth.

## Technical & Personal Reflections

- **Responsibility Boundaries**: Modernization does not mean replacing Bash with Python or adding types for their own sake. It means establishing clear boundaries around responsibilities that were previously mixed together (domain logic, process orchestration, OS execution, secret access) and making those boundaries enforceable and testable.
- **Discipline of Investigation**: In client discovery, separating verifiable ground truth from interpretations prevents prescribing solutions to problems the business does not actually have.
- **The Ledger vs. Memory Pattern**: In learning systems and data pipelines, never mutate an immutable event log to track consumer state; maintain consumer provenance explicitly as metadata in downstream artifacts.
