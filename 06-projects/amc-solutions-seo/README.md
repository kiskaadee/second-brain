# AMC Solutions — Digital Discovery

## Overview

Research into AMC Solutions Colombia's digital presence to establish the current state of its website, user experience, search visibility, and external presence. The work gathers evidence to inform a future proposal for redesign, SEO, and customer acquisition; it does not include implementing those initiatives.

## Research question

What changes does AMC Solutions Colombia's digital presence need to serve as a channel for customer acquisition and market positioning?

## Scope

- Inventory of website content, navigation, and calls to action.
- Inspection of the technical implementation, HTTP responses, technologies, and crawlability.
- Analysis of information architecture, user experience, and contact points.
- Review of metadata, structured data, and search engine readiness.
- Research into external presence on Google Business Profile, LinkedIn, and industry directories.

CMS selection, redesign, final copywriting, and advertising budget decisions are outside the current scope.

## Research organization

The repository separates context and unknowns, empirical evidence, analysis, deliverables, and inspection tools. Observations should link to their supporting evidence and distinguish facts from interpretations and hypotheses.

```text
context/   Background, hypotheses, unknowns, and questions for AMC
evidence/  Website, technical, and external presence records
analysis/  Interpretation of UX, SEO, search, and positioning
output/    Reports and materials prepared for AMC
tools/     Reproducible technical inspection scripts
```
